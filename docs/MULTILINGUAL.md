# Ukrainian and English storefront

Ukrainian is the default language. Visitors select **EN** in the header; the selected English view is carried in internal storefront URLs as `?lang=en`.

- Catalog, categories, products, cart and checkout use one WooCommerce record set. Prices, stock and SKU therefore never diverge by language.
- The theme supplies English interface text for the public storefront. Keep product names and technical statements unchanged until their English wording is approved.
- To add approved English product copy, use the product’s short description/description only after it is verified by the brand team. Do not invent specifications in either language.
- The language switch is part of the theme; no paid multilingual or WooCommerce extension is required.

Before publishing, check the main navigation, a category, a product, cart and checkout in both languages after setting the final production domain.
